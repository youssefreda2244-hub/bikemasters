Since 2018

Built by riders,
for riders.

Bike Masters started as a small counter in Zahraa El Maadi and grew into one of Cairo's go-to shops for road cycling — because we buy, service, and ride the same bikes we sell.

We also organize group rides for the local community, so if you're new to the road scene in Cairo, our shop is a reasonable place to start.
